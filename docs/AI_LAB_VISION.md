# AI Lab — Creative Vision

## Document status

- Status: Initial working vision.
- Project: Emad.dev AI Lab.
- Creative owner: Emad Kadyear.
- Central aesthetic selected: Cosmic anomaly.
- This document will evolve as decisions are approved.

## 1. Purpose

AI Lab will be a second portfolio experience connected to Emad's existing professional portfolio.

Its purpose is not to replace the original portfolio. Its purpose is to explore how far a personal website can be transformed through artificial intelligence, 3D graphics, cinematic animation and modern browser technologies.

The complete work should demonstrate two complementary abilities:

1. Emad can design and develop a clear professional portfolio manually.
2. Emad can direct an ambitious AI-assisted creative and technical project while understanding, reviewing and documenting its construction.

## 2. Core concept

The existing portfolio represents a stable digital reality created manually.

A button named **ACTIVATE AI LAB** allows the visitor to break that reality. When activated, the existing interface loses its physical stability, is absorbed by a singularity and is reborn as an explorable 3D universe.

The central visual law is **gravity**:

- Text bends toward the singularity.
- Cards detach and become physical fragments.
- Images stretch and distort.
- Interface elements orbit before disappearing.
- Project worlds revolve around a central anomaly.
- Movement, navigation and transitions feel gravitational.

The experience should feel excessive, surprising and liberating, but never arbitrary.

## 3. Central message

The narrative is a comparison between two ways of creating:

- The original portfolio was designed and developed manually by Emad.
- AI Lab is creatively directed by Emad and developed with AI assistance.

Possible main statement:

> First I built my portfolio with code. Then I wanted to discover how far artificial intelligence could transform it.

Possible message after the transformation:

> You have left the handcrafted portfolio. Welcome to the singularity created with AI.

The final wording will be written in Spanish and English through the existing internationalization system.

## 4. Experience architecture

The complete experience has three acts.

### Act I — Stable reality

The visitor enters the existing portfolio.

It remains:

- Professional.
- Fast.
- Clear.
- Accessible.
- Fully navigable.
- Available in Spanish and English.
- Compatible with the existing themes.

The original content must be understandable without activating AI Lab.

### Act II — The transformation

The visitor voluntarily activates AI Lab.

Target cinematic sequence:

1. The button changes state and confirms activation.
2. The interface starts showing subtle visual instability.
3. Text and cards bend toward a point in space.
4. Sections separate into fragments.
5. The fragments begin orbiting a black singularity.
6. The entire original portfolio collapses into a nearly invisible point.
7. The screen remains dark for a short dramatic pause.
8. A blue-white explosion creates the new universe.
9. The camera stabilizes in front of the AI Lab hub.

The final target duration should be approximately 6–8 seconds, but it must include:

- A visible skip control.
- A reduced-motion version.
- A shorter mobile version.
- Safe recovery if loading fails.

The first technical prototype will not attempt the final effect. It will validate the state flow and navigation with a simple transition.

### Act III — The AI universe

The visitor arrives in a cosmic space organized around a central singularity.

The singularity acts as the visual and narrative core. Project worlds, skill constellations, trajectory signals and communication objects orbit around it.

The experience should communicate scale and freedom without forcing the visitor to learn game controls.

## 5. The central universe

Current recommended concept:

- A black cosmic environment.
- Blue light inherited from the current Emad.dev identity.
- A physically convincing black hole or gravitational core.
- Slow-moving debris originating from the original portfolio.
- A monumental Emad.dev monolith near the singularity.
- Project worlds placed as recognizable destinations.
- A restrained holographic interface containing essential navigation.

The universe should initially appear calm after the violent transformation. This contrast gives the explosion more impact and prevents permanent visual exhaustion.

## 6. Navigation

The visitor should never be required to move like a videogame character.

Recommended interaction:

- Mouse or touch movement creates subtle camera parallax.
- Clicking a world triggers a controlled camera journey.
- Keyboard focus can select the same destinations.
- A persistent interface provides direct access to all sections.
- A visible control returns to the original portfolio.

Essential destinations:

- Projects.
- Skills.
- Professional journey.
- CV.
- Contact.
- AI creation log.

The 3D objects enrich navigation; they do not replace accessible HTML controls.

## 7. Project worlds

Only real projects belonging to Emad may be represented.

### 7.1 First Portfolio — The origin archive

Narrative role:

- Represents the beginning of Emad's portfolio evolution.
- Preserves the previous visual identity as a digital relic.

Possible world:

- An abandoned orbital archive.
- Fragments of the first interface preserved behind glass.
- Angular and GSAP represented as moving structural mechanisms.

Information shown:

- Project purpose.
- What Emad learned.
- Technologies used.
- Live project link.
- Repository link when available.

### 7.2 Temple of Fight — The eclipsed temple

Narrative role:

- Connects Emad's experience in combat sports with motion-oriented frontend development.

Possible world:

- A temple or combat arena floating before an eclipse.
- AI-generated gloves, masks, statues or training objects.
- Controlled impact animations using GSAP.
- Energy moving through the architecture when the project is selected.

The scene must remain professional and avoid looking like an unrelated videogame demo.

### 7.3 Laboratorio GeoControl — The stratified planet

Narrative role:

- Represents a real internship project and practical work for an external organization.

Possible world:

- A planet or geological core divided into visible layers.
- The visitor descends through strata representing discovery, design, WordPress implementation and delivery.
- Soil samples, instruments or structural elements may be generated as 3D assets.

The case study should emphasize real requirements, client work and completed delivery.

### 7.4 JS Quiz — The logic constellation

Narrative role:

- Represents Full-Stack architecture and the connection between interface, backend and database.

Possible world:

- A logical cube or artificial brain.
- Questions travel as electrical signals.
- Three connected layers represent Angular, NestJS and MySQL.
- Selecting a layer reveals its responsibility in the application.

The visualization should explain architecture without inventing functionality.

## 8. Skills

Skills will not be shown only as a wall of logos.

Possible representation:

- Frontend technologies form one constellation.
- Backend technologies form a second constellation.
- Data technologies form a structured orbital system.
- Deployment and DevOps tools appear as communication satellites.

Selecting a technology should show where Emad has actually used it. Do not imply professional mastery that is not supported by the portfolio or CV.

## 9. Professional journey

Emad's previous career is not hidden. It becomes the reason behind the concept of discipline, transformation and progression.

The journey may be represented as a trail through space:

1. RZGym — coordination, operations, organization and customer service.
2. Team Black Panthers — entrepreneurship, complete business management and trainer coordination.
3. Personal training — discipline, teaching and independent working method.
4. Web development training — transition into software development.
5. Full-Stack projects — application of technical learning.
6. Future direction — backend, databases and DevOps growth.

The key message is:

> Emad did not start from zero. He changed arenas while carrying years of discipline, coordination and responsibility.

## 10. CV and contact

### CV

The CV may appear as a floating data capsule, but the actual files must remain normal downloadable links:

- Spanish CV.
- English CV.

### Contact

The contact area may be represented as a communication beacon.

Sending a message could visually launch a signal toward the central singularity. The form itself must remain an accessible HTML form with clear success and error states.

LinkedIn and GitHub must remain directly accessible.

## 11. AI creation log

AI Lab should include a transparent creation section explaining:

- Creative direction by Emad.
- Development assistance from Claude.
- 3D generation with Meshy where applicable.
- Manual review and decisions.
- Asset optimization.
- Accessibility and performance decisions.
- Problems found and how they were resolved.
- Tools considered but rejected and why.

This section turns the use of AI into a documented professional process rather than a hidden shortcut.

## 12. Visual direction

### Preserve from the original identity

- Dark navy foundations.
- Electric blue accent.
- Clean typography for professional information.
- Emad.dev name and identity.
- Clear hierarchy and restrained interface controls.

### Expand inside AI Lab

- Deep black space.
- Blue-white gravitational light.
- Volumetric atmosphere.
- Monoliths and monumental scale.
- Controlled lens distortion.
- Particles derived from interface fragments.
- Slow orbital motion.
- Occasional high-impact cinematic transitions.

The universe must not become a random mixture of unrelated styles. Every world can be distinctive, but all worlds belong to the same cosmic system.

## 13. Sound

Sound is optional and disabled by default.

Possible uses:

- Low gravitational ambience.
- Subtle interface signals.
- Short impact during the collapse.
- Explosion and arrival sound.
- Unique ambience for each project world.

Requirements:

- Never autoplay sound.
- Provide a clear sound toggle.
- Remember the user's choice only through an approved local preference.
- Do not make essential information depend on sound.

## 14. Motion and accessibility

- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Provide a non-distorted fade transition for reduced motion.
- All destinations must be keyboard accessible.
- Visible focus states must be preserved.
- Essential text must remain HTML, not a texture inside WebGL.
- Interactive 3D objects must have equivalent labeled controls.
- Do not use rapid flashes.
- Avoid camera motion that may cause dizziness.
- Provide a way to skip cinematics.

## 15. Performance principles

- Do not load AI Lab during the first render of the original portfolio.
- Load the central hub before optional project worlds.
- Load one project world only when needed.
- Use GLB for 3D delivery unless a specific exception is approved.
- Compress geometry and textures.
- Use multiple levels of detail where useful.
- Use procedural effects instead of large video files when practical.
- Reduce particles, resolution and postprocessing on mobile.
- Provide a static or lightweight fallback if WebGL is unavailable.

## 16. Non-goals

AI Lab is not:

- A replacement for the original portfolio.
- A full videogame.
- A mandatory intro before seeing professional information.
- A reason to hide CV or contact details.
- A showcase of random effects without meaning.
- A claim that all work was manually coded.
- A reason to install every available 3D library.

## 17. Success criteria

AI Lab will be successful if:

- The original portfolio remains intact and fast.
- The transformation produces a memorable first impression.
- Visitors understand the difference between the handcrafted and AI-assisted experiences.
- Every project world communicates real information.
- The experience remains navigable without understanding 3D controls.
- Mobile and reduced-motion visitors receive a coherent experience.
- AI involvement is documented clearly.
- The project demonstrates Emad's creative direction and technical judgment.
